# Westruun — City Map (Arcano Lab)

The city of Westruun (Tal'Dorei) for Foundry VTT v14 and D&D 5e, in English and Brazilian Portuguese. **Beta 1.0.1**.

- 16k city map with a title, ward and gate names, and a name for every location that the GM shows or hides one by one
- A journal for every ward and location: description for players, GM guide, hooks and rumors
- One ready scene for every illustration, with **Activate scene** buttons inside the journal
- 56 NPCs with sheet (SRD base), portrait and token
- Street, landmark and house lights that turn on at night, following the world time
- Clouds over the city, rain and fog (FXMaster), lightning over Greystone Tower (Sequencer + JB2A)
- Ambient sounds and 5 playlists organized by moment of play
- GM panel: what players see, house lights, lightning, organize journals, update and repair

## Install
Paste this manifest in Foundry (Add-on Modules → Install Module):

`https://github.com/arcanolab/arcano-westruun/releases/latest/download/module.json`

Foundry also installs **Arcano Lab Hub**, which adds the Arcano Lab button where the Westruun panel opens. Recommended: FXMaster, Sequencer and JB2A.

## Use
1. Enable the module in your world.
2. Open the compendium **Westruun (EN)** or **Westruun (PT-BR)**, open the Adventure and click **Import**. The module builds the city in about a minute.
3. Open the GM panel from the Arcano Lab button on the scene controls.

Already imported an older version? Open the panel and click **Update and repair**.

The language of the module screens can be set in Configure Settings → Westruun (Follow Foundry, Português or English).
